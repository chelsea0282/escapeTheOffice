# Design Notes

## Framing
This game is built around a simple but unsettling premise: the player wants to leave, but the world does not treat that desire as self-evident. Instead, the player must make their intention legible to others.

## Agency as negotiation
The work is not about whether the player can leave. The work is about whether the player can justify leaving in a system that is already interpreting them.

This creates a tension between:
- internal desire
- external explanation
- social legitimacy
- institutional evaluation

## Why the office matters
The office setting is useful because it already contains built-in incentives, norms, and rituals. Everyone is reasonable. Everyone is acting in good faith. Yet the structure of the environment makes ordinary exits unusually difficult.

## Connection to the NeurIPS theme
The piece asks whether agency is something you simply possess, or something you must continually defend and translate into a form that others can accept.

It also explores whether agency becomes more meaningful when it is clarified through justification, or whether it is weakened when it must be made legible to systems that may misread it.

## Tone
The tone should be dry, everyday, and slightly uncanny. The office should feel normal until the pressure of explanation begins to distort it.
