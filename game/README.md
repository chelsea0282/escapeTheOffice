# Escape the Office

## Concept
A text-based negotiation game about trying to leave work on time while being evaluated by an office system that asks for justification at every turn.

## Core premise
The player wants to leave at 5:00 PM. That desire is ordinary, but in this world it is not enough on its own. Every action must be rendered legible to others: coworkers, managers, and an increasingly judgmental system.

## Themes
- agency under constraint
- the pressure to justify yourself
- workplace incentives and social expectation
- the strange intimacy of being evaluated by a system

## Suggested structure
1. Opening scene at 4:47 PM
2. First interruption from a manager or coworker
3. A negotiation over what counts as a reasonable reason
4. A second request that intensifies the pressure
5. A final exit scene with a hidden evaluation or consequence
